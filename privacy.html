Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dakhilaa Private Limited ("Dakhilaa", "we", "us", or "our"), a company incorporated in India, with its registered office at Chini Mill, Gajadhar Ganj, Buxar, Bihar 802103, India collects, uses, and protects your information when you use www.dakhilaa.com and its services. By using the Platform, you agree to this Policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details you provide: name, mobile number, and (optionally) email and social handles.
  • Usage data: tests attempted, answers, scores, progress, and leaderboard entries.
  • Partner/payout details (for partners only): the details you enter for verification and payouts.
  • Payment data: handled securely by our payment partner (Razorpay). We receive a payment confirmation but do not store your card or bank numbers.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and run your account and deliver the services.
  • To show your progress, weak-topic reports, scores, and leaderboard rank.
  • To process payments and, for partners, to calculate and pay commissions.
  • To improve the Platform and provide support.
  • To send important service messages. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored securely using trusted third-party providers, including Google Firebase (authentication and database), Vercel (hosting), and Razorpay (payments). These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments.

4. Sensitive details (Aadhaar / PAN)

Partners may provide Aadhaar and PAN details for payout verification. These are used only for identity verification and payouts, are accessible only to authorised administrators, and are never shared publicly or used for any other purpose.

5. Cookies & local storage

We use browser storage and similar technologies to keep you logged in and remember your preferences and referral code. You can clear these from your browser at any time.

6. Sharing

We share information only with the service providers listed above (to run the Platform) or where required by law. Your name and score may appear on the live-test leaderboard to other users; you can choose not to attempt the live test if you prefer not to appear.

7. Your choices

You can view and update your profile in the app. To request deletion of your account and associated data, email support@dakhilaa.com. Some records may be retained where required for legal or accounting reasons.

8. Children

Many of our users are students under 18. We collect only the minimum information needed to provide the service and recommend that younger students use the Platform with parental involvement.

9. Changes & contact

We may update this Policy; the latest version is always posted here. For any privacy question or request, contact support@dakhilaa.com.
