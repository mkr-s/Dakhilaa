How to Score High in JEE Main Physics

Mukesh Singh·22 Oct 2026·6 min read

Physics is where ranks are won and lost. It is conceptual enough to reward real understanding and tricky enough to punish rote learners — which is good news if you prepare the right way.

Prioritise the heavy chapters

A handful of chapters carry most of the Physics marks every year. Get Electrodynamics, Mechanics and Modern Physics solid before you polish low-yield topics.

Lock in the heavy-weight Physics chapters before chasing the rest.

Concepts first, then problems — lots of them

Understand the concept, then drill problems — Physics rewards application, not reading.

Reading theory feels productive but rarely moves your Physics score. Understand a concept once, then spend the bulk of your time solving varied problems on it.

Stop the avoidable losses

Most lost Physics marks are unit, sign and misreading errors — all fixable.
In Physics, the student who understands 20 chapters deeply beats the one who has "seen" all 30.
