Last 30 Days Before JEE Main: A Weak-Topic-First Revision Plan

Mukesh Singh·26 Sep 2026·6 min read

The last month doesn't build new ability — it converts what you already have into marks. Most students waste it re-reading comfortable chapters. Here is how to spend it so it actually lifts your rank.

The 30-day shape

Front-load weak topics, build through mocks, then taper — don't cram to the last day.
  • Days 1–10: attack your weakest high-weight topics with problems.
  • Days 11–20: full-length mocks every 2–3 days, with deep analysis.
  • Days 21–27: targeted revision of mistakes and formula sheets.
  • Days 28–30: light revision, full sleep, no new topics.

What a focused day looks like

About 8 sharp, structured hours beats 14 tired ones.

Note the split: more time on weak topics and mock analysis than on passive revision. And rest is on the chart on purpose — a fried brain forgets what a rested one retains.

Ramp the mocks

Build your mock count steadily so you peak in real exam conditions.
Take mocks at the real time of day your exam is scheduled. You are training your brain's rhythm, not just your knowledge.
