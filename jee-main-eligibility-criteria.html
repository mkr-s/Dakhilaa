JEE Main 2026 Eligibility Criteria, Explained Simply

Mukesh Singh·16 Oct 2026·6 min read

Before you plan your prep, make sure you actually qualify to sit the exam. JEE Main eligibility is simpler than rumours suggest — here are the essentials. One rule above all: confirm the exact criteria in the official NTA information bulletin for your year, because details can change.

The core requirements

The core boxes to tick before you apply for JEE Main.
  • You must have passed (or be appearing in) Class 12 or equivalent with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics.
  • There is generally no minimum percentage just to appear — but top institutes have their own admission cut-offs.
  • Number of subjects and specific combinations are defined by NTA — check the bulletin.

Attempts and age

JEE Main is open across three consecutive years — droppers included.

JEE Main can be attempted across three consecutive years, with two sessions each year. There is generally no upper age limit just to appear, though individual institutes may apply their own rules.

Good news for droppers

A drop year does not cost you eligibility — droppers can appear.
A drop year does not disqualify you. If you are within the attempt window, you are eligible — your job is simply to improve.
