JEE Main vs JEE Advanced: Key Differences You Must Know

Mukesh Singh·14 Oct 2026·6 min read

Many aspirants use "JEE" as one word, but Main and Advanced are two different exams with different purposes. Getting the distinction clear early changes how you plan.

Who each exam is for

JEE Main is your gateway to NITs, IIITs and GFTIs — and it is the screening test for JEE Advanced. JEE Advanced is only for admission to the IITs, and only the top performers in Main can sit for it.

Main and Advanced differ in purpose, colleges, pattern and attempts.

Difficulty and pattern

Main rewards speed and accuracy on a fixed, predictable pattern. Advanced is deliberately unpredictable — the pattern changes year to year, questions are multi-concept, and depth matters more than speed.

Advanced goes deeper; Main rewards clean, fast execution.

Attempts and how Main leads to Advanced

  • JEE Main: held twice a year; you may attempt it across three consecutive years.
  • JEE Advanced: once a year; a maximum of two attempts in consecutive years.
  • Only a limited top slice of Main qualifiers (announced each year) can appear for Advanced.
Main is the funnel: only the top qualifiers reach Advanced.
You cannot skip Main. A strong, stable Main score is the ticket that even lets you attempt Advanced.
