JEE Main Maths: How to Build Speed and Accuracy

Mukesh Singh·26 Oct 2026·6 min read

Maths is the subject that most often wrecks time management. A few long questions can eat the clock you needed for Physics and Chemistry. The fix is deliberate practice for both speed and accuracy.

Speed and accuracy are both trainable

The target is the top-right: fast AND accurate — both are trainable.

Speed without accuracy wastes negative marks; accuracy without speed costs you questions you never reach. You need both, and timed practice is how you build them together.

Know where the marks live

Calculus and Coordinate Geometry carry the most Maths marks — prioritise them.

Calculus leads Maths by a distance, followed by Coordinate Geometry and Algebra. Being fast in a low-weight chapter helps little; be fast where the marks are.

Practise timed, analyse hard

Practise against a clock, then review every slow or wrong question.
In Maths, an untimed solve teaches you the method. A timed solve teaches you the exam.
