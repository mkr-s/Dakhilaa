Best Books for JEE Main Preparation (Physics, Chemistry & Maths)

Mukesh Singh·20 Oct 2026·6 min read

Students collect books like trophies and read none of them deeply. For JEE Main, a short, well-used shelf beats a tall, untouched one. Here is what actually matters.

NCERT first — especially for Chemistry

NCERT is non-negotiable, and for Chemistry it is practically the exam itself — a large share of Inorganic and Physical questions come straight from its lines. Master NCERT before touching anything thicker.

Start from NCERT, then add one trusted reference per subject — not five.

One strong reference per subject

  • Physics: NCERT for concepts, then a problem-focused reference for practice.
  • Chemistry: NCERT as the base, plus a concise reference for numericals and Organic mechanisms.
  • Maths: a single rigorous problem book you actually finish beats three you sample.
More books do not mean more marks — depth in a few does.

How to actually use a book

A book only works inside a loop: read, solve, log mistakes, revisit.
A book you finish and revise beats three you start. Depth, not shelf length, is what the exam rewards.
